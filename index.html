    About AFAGS

    The Amateur Fencing Association of Gujarat State (AFAGS) is the governing body for fencing in Gujarat, affiliated to the Fencing Association of India. We promote, regulate and develop the sport across the state, working with district units, clubs, coaches, referees and athletes to build a strong fencing ecosystem.
